## About:
##  <img src="Images & Icons/helpbuddy-icon.png" width="30px" alt="icon">HelpBuddy

**HelpBuddy** is a clean, multi-page web platform designed to provide aspiring developers and students with instant access to high-quality, free learning materials. Built using pure **HTML5** and **CSS3**, HelpBuddy cuts through the noise of scattered internet searches by organizing top-tier learning content into one central hub.

---

## 🗺️ Page-by-Page Overview & Features

### 🏠 1. Home Page
<p align="center">
  <img src="Images & Icons/home-screenshot.png" width="80%" alt="HelpBuddy Home Page">
</p>


- 🎯 **Categorized Course Hub:** Organized containers for Programming Languages, Databases, Web Development Frameworks, and Libraries.
- ▶️ **Direct Video Access:** Click **"Start Watching"** on available cards to jump straight to curated YouTube tutorials and playlists.
- ⏳ **Content Status:** Direct links are currently active for featured courses, with links for remaining topics being integrated progressively.
